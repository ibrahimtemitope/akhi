Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: name, email, company name, and details about your AI systems. We also collect engagement data through forms and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: (a) provide and improve our services, (b) contact you about engagements, (c) send newsletters (if opted in), (d) comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Security

We store all data in Supabase with encryption. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only. We do not sell or share your data with third parties without consent.

4. Newsletter & Communications

If you subscribe to our newsletter, you can unsubscribe anytime by clicking the unsubscribe link in emails. We respect your communication preferences.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Supabase (database) and Vercel (hosting). These providers have their own privacy policies. We do not share personal data beyond what's necessary for service delivery.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal tracking. We do not use cookies for advertising. Analytics are basic (page views, bounce rate). No personal identifiers are tracked.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to: (a) access your data, (b) correct inaccurate data, (c) request deletion of your data, (d) opt-out of communications. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations. You can request deletion anytime.

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. We'll notify you of significant changes via email or website notice.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests: akhi-security@tasknory.com
